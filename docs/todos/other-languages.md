# Other languages

English first. ARIA speaks French only today.

Parakeet and Smart Turn are multilingual. These are not:

- the calibration utterances: they rest on French function words, so they must be **written**, not translated;
- the phonetic folding of the name, the list of words that lead on (`turn::LEADS_ON`), the sleep phrases;
- the interface, the prompts, the wake-word model.
